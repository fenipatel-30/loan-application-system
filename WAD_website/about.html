About Us

A simple online portal for submitting and tracking loan applications.

Making the application process easier

The Loan Application System brings the application process into one place. Applicants can create an account, provide their loan details, and follow the status of their applications from their profile.

Applications are reviewed by the lending team. A submitted application is not a loan approval; the final decision and any related details are shown when the application status is updated.
